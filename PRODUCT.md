# Medina's Airwork — product

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
1. **Homeowners in and around Philadelphia** who need heat or AC fixed, replaced or installed — mostly arriving on a phone from a Facebook link, often in Facebook's in-app browser. Heating season (October onward) is the moment.
2. **Other HVAC contractors** who need installation help or want to team up on projects (the owner introduced the business in a contractors' trade group, so a contractor may be the first visitor).
3. **Miguel, the owner** — the site is given to him for free; it must win him over within one phone screen.

## Purpose
Turn a visitor into a phone call or a free-estimate text to Miguel. Give the business its first real web presence (it currently has none: no site, no Maps listing, no reviews).

## Positioning
A Philadelphia heating and cooling business run by its owner, Miguel. Owner's own tagline (flyer): "Heating & Cooling Solutions You Can Trust." Distinctive: willing to travel long distances for installs ("over-the-road" install work) and supports other contractors.

- Flyer also lists: air conditioning, heating, air handlers, heat pump systems, mini-split systems (single & multi-zone), professional service repairs, preventative maintenance, water heaters, gas furnaces.
- Service calls and repairs within about an hour of Philadelphia. Flyer: "Serving Philadelphia, Delaware, Bucks, and Montgomery County." Installs: long distance.
- Contact: phone 215-839-2766 (texts: unknown), email medinas.airwork@gmail.com. No booking system.
- Unknown: hours, prices, years in business, surname, street address.
